## Vehicle cover

Your policy covers your car, van or motorbike when a kaiju damages it, for example a roof crushed by a footstep
or a car thrown into the harbour. The vehicle must be parked at the address in your policy schedule or on a public
street of the city when the damage happens.

## Building cover

Your policy covers the building you live in: walls, roof, windows, foundations and fixed installations damaged by
a kaiju, for example by a tail strike. Repairs are paid up to the rebuild value in your policy schedule.

## Contents cover

Your policy covers the contents of your home: furniture, clothes, electronics and other belongings damaged or
destroyed by a kaiju, including damage from collapsing ceilings and from flooding caused by the kaiju.

## Garden cover

Your policy covers your garden: lawns, hedges, trees, sheds, fences and greenhouses trampled or flattened by a
kaiju. Plants are replaced with young plants of the same kind.
