## How to report a claim

Report a claim through the claims desk chat as soon as it is safe. Describe the incident: where and when the kaiju
appeared, how large it was and what it damaged. An adjuster reviews every claim before anything is paid.

## Deductible

For every claim you pay the deductible yourself: the amount in your policy schedule. The deductible is taken off
the payout once per incident, even when several damage items are claimed.

## Proof of damage

Send photos of every damaged item and receipts or estimates for the repairs. A sighting bulletin or a news report
of the incident helps the adjuster. Without proof of damage a claim cannot be approved.
