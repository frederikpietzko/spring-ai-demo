## Act of Kaiju exclusion

The Act of Kaiju exclusion: damage from a class 5 kaiju event is not covered. A class 5 kaiju event is a
city-level event; damage to your car, building, contents or garden during such an event is excluded from every
coverage of your policy. Damage from kaiju events of class 1 to 4 stays covered.

## Provoking the kaiju exclusion

The provoking the kaiju exclusion: damage caused by the policyholder provoking the kaiju is not covered. Provoking
means deliberately attracting or attacking the kaiju, for example with fireworks, floodlights or loud music aimed
at it.

## Wear and tear

The wear and tear exclusion: rust, rot and damage from age or gradual deterioration are not covered, even when a
kaiju later finishes the job.

## Wilful damage

The wilful damage exclusion: damage that the policyholder causes on purpose, or arranges with others, is not
covered, and a claim for it is rejected.
