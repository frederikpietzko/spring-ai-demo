# Kaiju classification

A kaiju class rates the severity of one kaiju event, from 1 to 5. Take the class from the claim; never raise or lower it yourself.

| Class | What it looks like |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | A large lizard. Scratches, a toppled bin, a trampled flower bed. |
| 2 | A kaiju the size of a bus. Dented cars, broken fences and windows. |
| 3 | A kaiju the size of a house. Crushed cars, damaged walls and roofs. |
| 4 | A kaiju taller than the old town hall. Collapsed buildings in one district. |
| 5 | A city-level kaiju. Damage across several districts at once. |

A class 5 event is an Act of Kaiju and the policy wording excludes it from coverage.

How to check a class:

1. Compare the damage items of the claim with the table. A class 1 kaiju does not crush a building.
2. If the damage is far above the class, say so and suggest a closer review instead of paying out.
3. Damage from class 1 to 4 events stays covered by the policy wording.
